Problem Framing Challenger

A short engineering design conversation tool

About this tool

Slow down the leap from “we think” to “therefore.”

The challenger examines the language and boundaries of a problem statement, then gives your team four to six questions to discuss.

It will not rewrite the statement, choose a problem, propose solutions, or grade your work. Your team decides what changes.
